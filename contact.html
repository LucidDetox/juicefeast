Common Questions

Before You Reach Out

How quickly will you respond?

We typically respond within a few hours during the day. If you message in the evening, expect a reply the following morning.

Is the detox consultation really free?

Yes, completely. The consultation exists so we can both assess whether the program is the right fit. There is no pitch and no obligation to proceed.

Can I do the detox program remotely if I'm not in Sayulita?

Yes. The full regenerative detox program is available to clients anywhere in the world via video consultation and WhatsApp support. The juice feast delivery is specific to Sayulita, but the protocol and guidance is available globally.

Can I combine the Juice Feast with the Detox program?

Yes, and it can be very effective. The juice feast can be incorporated as part of a personalized detox protocol. Mention this in your consultation and we'll design it accordingly.

What's the best way to contact you?

WhatsApp is fastest and most reliable. You can also email us at hello@sayulitadetox.com for less time-sensitive inquiries.
