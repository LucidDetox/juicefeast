Since 2012, we've been helping people reclaim their health through the transformative power of regenerative detoxification, one body at a time.

2012
How It Began

A Mission Born
in 2012.

The mission began in 2012 with a single conviction: that the body has a profound capacity to heal itself, and that most people are never given the right conditions to experience it.

Over the years the practice found its true home in Sayulita, one of Mexico's most vibrant coastal villages, where the environment itself became part of the work. Today it serves guests in Sayulita and clients around the world through personalized, specialist-led programs.

Over the years we've worked with hundreds of people dealing with chronic fatigue, digestive disorders, hormonal imbalances, inflammation, brain fog, and the general accumulation of modern life. The results, again and again, have confirmed what we believed from the beginning: a good detox program is the antidote to modern living and the foundation of health.

Why Sayulita

The Healing Power of Place

There is something about Sayulita that accelerates the process. The clean ocean air, the sun, the jungle, the slower rhythm of life, the absence of the relentless stimulation of modern cities.

Our in-person clients consistently report that the environment itself becomes part of the healing. The body relaxes in ways it simply can't in most urban settings, and the detoxification process deepens as a result.

For those who can't make it to Sayulita, we bring everything we know to you, remotely, with the same depth of care and personalisation.
